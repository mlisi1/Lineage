# Chronicle and bestiary

## Chronicle (M9)

Event detectors turn world changes into dated entries with templated text and an
importance score: speciation, extinction, firsts, records, booms and crashes, migrations,
landmass splits and rejoins, fires, volcanoes, god powers, anniversaries. Entries are
deduplicated and rate limited.

Targets: `[2–15]` notable entries per sim-month (median); no event type above 30% of all
entries.

## Bestiary (M9)

Every species that ever lived gets a record with a genome snapshot for its portrait,
dates, ancestry and notable facts. Bestiaries are archived with their world (M10).

## Outputs

Captions on the desk screen (R3), push notifications for major events (S4), the phone app
(S3) and the daily journal (S5).
