What was measured

Measured on a display standing in for the 7″ screen: the page was 2560×1440, 6.0 times the pixels of the 1024×600 screen: at least as hard to draw. The scene is drawn at the same size and stretched further.

Each browser profile starts Chromium afresh with different flags and runs every scenario. The page counts frames itself; the runner samples Chromium's processes from /proc meanwhile. Frame rate cannot exceed the display's refresh rate. “Drawn at” is the size the scene is rendered at before being stretched to the screen with hard pixel edges: smaller means bigger art pixels and less work.
