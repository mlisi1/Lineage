  • Where species come from. Per inhabited sim-month the median seed sees 1.00 splits and 0.36 arrivals, and loses 1.12 species. 100% of seeds saw at least one split. Divergence on the island makes more species than arrivals do.
  • Splits come in bursts. The largest burst of a seed (daughters of one species born within 30 days) has a median of 6 and reaches 13: when a herd has spread wide enough, it breaks into several clusters within weeks rather than one at a time. Species are clusters a little coarser than parent and young differ (ADR 0012), so their number follows how widely the herd has spread genetically.
  • How long they last. Arrived species live a median of 29 days, species born by a split 110 days.
