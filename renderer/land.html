The turn of the year

The same wood from the middle view, on five days around three changes of season. Places turn one after another over about four weeks: in fronts across the land, the hills first into autumn and winter and last into spring, and each tree on its own day near its place's. Where the stream carries the plants' EVERGREEN gene, the more of it, the later a tree turns (the R0 fixture has no genes, so here every tree turns by place and chance).
